A · Ink & Brass

Recommended

Neutral-warm darks, ivory text, brass as the single brand accent. Green is demoted to a purely functional "gain" colour — it never appears as decoration. Gold reads as stored wealth, which in Pakistan is literal: gold is the default savings asset. Nothing about it looks generated.

B · Paper & Ledger

Boldest

Light-first. Warm paper ground, near-black ink, deep flag-green as brand, burnt ochre as the second voice. Reads like a private wealth manager's statement rather than a crypto dashboard. Effectively zero AI-generated landing pages go light-first premium, so this is the strongest differentiator — and the hardest to keep looking rich.

C · Midnight & Saffron

Warmest

Deep indigo-black grounds with a saffron/marigold accent. Keeps the dark drama of your reference but swaps the mint for a warm accent and untints the neutrals toward indigo instead of green. Subcontinental warmth without tipping into kitsch.
